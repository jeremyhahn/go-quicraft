# #917: Quiesce on a real three-replica shard

Refs #744 (v1.0.0 bar test gap).

## Objective

`Config.Quiesce` is documented in docs/raft-consensus/user.md (Quiesce) and
docs/raft-consensus/engineering.md section 10, but it is tested only inside
pkg/internal/raft. Prove the documented behaviour end to end on three Hosts
over the real QUIC transport.

Documented behaviour under test (one election timeout = ElectionRTT ticks):

- a shard idle for 10 election timeouts quiesces: the leader replaces
  heartbeats with liveness probes every 10 election timeouts (the liveness
  interval) and CheckQuorum keeps running;
- quiesced followers use an election timeout of 3 liveness intervals;
- the next proposal, read, config change or inbound replication wakes it.

## Slices

1. `docs: plan for #917`: this file.
2. `test: an idle three-replica shard with Quiesce keeps its leader without
   elections and wakes on the next proposal`: new
   test/integration/host/quiesce_test.go (`//go:build integration`), three
   Hosts with an EventListener each recording OnCampaignLaunched and
   OnLeaderUpdated, shard started with Quiesce true. Assertions:
   - (a) after a leader and 10 applied proposals, 12 election timeouts of
     idleness launch no campaign on any Host and every Host reports the same
     leader;
   - (b) a proposal after the idle period applies on all three replicas
     within 5 election timeouts;
   - (c) after a further idle period a SyncRead on the leader returns the
     last write;
   - (d) after the shard is idle again, stopping the leader's Host lets the
     two survivors elect a new leader within 4 liveness intervals, and a
     proposal through it applies on both.
3. Per divergence from the docs: a failing `test:` commit, then a `fix:`
   commit in pkg/internal/raft/quiesce.go or raft.go; a wrong doc is
   corrected in docs/raft-consensus/engineering.md instead.

Waits poll against deadlines; the idle windows are the only fixed sleeps.

## Out of scope

- Config-change and inbound-replication wake paths (unit-tested in
  pkg/internal/raft).
- Partition behaviour under quiesce (CheckQuorum step-down).
- Any wire or on-disk format change.
