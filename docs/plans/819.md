# #819: an observer replica receives every entry and never counts toward quorum

Objective: an end-to-end test on an in-process, mTLS-connected shard of three
voters on three Hosts plus a fourth Host running an observer.

Slice 1 (this branch), `pkg/observer_replica_test.go`:
- Start three voters on the `kv` memory store; add replica 4 with
  `SyncRequestAddObserverWithIdentity`, then start it with `join=true` and
  `Config.IsObserver`.
- Propose puts; the observer's `StateMachineHash` reaches the leader's at the
  same applied index, and `StaleRead` on the observer returns the value.
- `SyncGetShardMembership` lists 4 in `Observers`, not in `Nodes`.
- Stop two voters: a proposal through the surviving voter fails (one voter
  plus an observer is no quorum), and the observer never reports itself
  leader in `LeaderIDAndTerm` or `ShardInfo`.

A bug found becomes a failing-test commit, then a fix in `pkg/host.go`.

Out of scope: observer promotion, witnesses, snapshots to observers, wire
or on-disk format changes.

## Review fix

- The quorum-loss step takes the survivor from `c.leader()` just before the
  stop (waits for a nonzero leader), closes the two other voters, and
  proposes through that leader and through the connected observer; every
  attempt must fail and the observer must never lead.
- The final `StaleRead` of "after" must fail with `*kv.KeyNotFoundError`
  (`errors.As`); nil or any other error fails the test.
- Test-only: if the observer is counted toward commit quorum, report it.

## Review fix 2

- CheckQuorum is off in `startVoters` and in the observer's shard config: the
  negative phase needs a leader that cannot step down.
- The quorum-loss step records the survivor's `ShardInfo` Term before the
  stop; a proposal failing with `ErrNotLeader` fails the test.
- After the proposals, the survivor still reports `IsLeader` in that Term and
  the observer reports `LeaderID` == survivor in that Term.

## Review fix 3

- One voter plus the observer is short of quorum either way, so the first
  test cannot catch an observer counted as a voter.
- `observerCluster.admitObserver` holds the admission and `StartShard` steps.
- New `TestObserverReplica_TwoVotersCommitWithObserverDown`: admit and catch
  up the observer, close it and one non-leader voter; a proposal through the
  leader commits (2 of 3 voters; 3 of 4 would not) and `StaleRead` on the
  other surviving voter returns it. Retries: 20 s total, 2 s per attempt.
