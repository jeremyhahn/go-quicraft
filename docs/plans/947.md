# Plan for #947: Checker.IsRevokedUnder test gap

Refs #744 (v1.0.0 bar test gap).

## Objective

`Checker.IsRevokedUnder` (pkg/internal/transport/revocation/checker.go)
resolves a leaf's issuer in the bundle it is given, not in the checker's
current issuer set, so a handshake verified against the bundle it started
under is judged against that bundle's issuer-scoped CRL entries. Its only
caller is the TLS verify path (pkg/internal/transport/tls.go); no test
calls it. Pin the documented behaviour with a test.

## Slices

1. `docs: plan for #947` — this file.
2. `test:` — pkg/internal/transport/revocation/revoked_under_test.go, CRL
   mode with CRL files from `createCRL`/`writeCRLFile` and leaves from
   `issueLeafWithAKI`:
   - (a) a CA A leaf whose serial A's CRL revokes is revoked under [A] and
     under [B, A];
   - (b) a CA B leaf with the same serial is not revoked under [A, B]: the
     scope follows the issuer, not the serial;
   - (c) the A leaf under a bundle with no issuer for it ([B], nil) gets the
     nil-issuer result: an `AddRevokedSerial` serial is revoked, the
     A-scoped serial is not;
   - (d) after `SetIssuers(B)` drops A, `IsRevokedUnder(leafA, [A])` still
     reports revoked while `IsRevoked(leafA)` does not;
   - (e) after `ReloadCRLs` with a CRL that no longer lists the serial,
     `IsRevokedUnder(leafA, [A])` reports not revoked.
3. Per divergence from the doc: a failing `test:` commit then a `fix:`
   commit in checker.go (or a doc-comment correction where the code is
   right).

## Out of scope

- OCSP, ModeBoth and ModeAny paths of IsRevokedUnder.
- Changes to the TLS caller or to any wire or on-disk format.
