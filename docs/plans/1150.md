## RemoveData layout conflict (vh1150aa)
- Choice: refuse, not remove both trees. removeDataSnapshotDir cross-checks GetSnapshot
  against the bootstrap DataDir's replica directory and returns the unexported
  *removeDataLayoutConflictError (matches ErrInvalidOperation) before the intent, retirement or delete.
  Removing both would need a multi-path intent and a delete of the old tree; deleting it
  before retirement can strand an unretired replica, and deleting it after drops the record
  that finds it on a crash. Refusal is reversible and the repair is documented on the error,
  RemoveData and api.md: start under the recorded DataDir, snapshot, stop, retry.
- A record in no known layout is now refused with a bootstrap record too.
- StartShard guard: unchanged. Under B (the recorded DataDir) the start is allowed, as at the
  base, since that is the repair path; under A it is refused by dataDirChangedError.
