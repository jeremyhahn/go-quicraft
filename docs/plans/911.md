# Plan for #911: WAL disk ceiling end to end

Refs #744 (v1.0.0 bar test gap).

## Objective

docs/multi-group-host/user.md and docs/log-storage/user.md promise that with
`HostConfig.MaxWALDiskSize` set, the WAL samples filesystem usage (statfs)
every 100 writes; at 95% of the ceiling `OnLogDBDiskFull` fires once with a
`DiskInfo`, new proposals fail with `ErrDiskFull`, the shard is not failed,
and `HealthCheck` reports `DiskOK` false. Only option construction is tested
today. Prove the whole path on a real WAL.

## Slices

1. `pkg/host_disk_ceiling_test.go` (`//go:build linux`),
   `TestDiskCeiling_RefusesProposalsFiresOnceAndKeepsAcknowledgedEntries`:
   a transportless Host on a real WAL in `t.TempDir()`, `MaxWALDiskSize`
   1<<20 (any real filesystem already uses more), a recording EventListener
   and a recording state machine.
   - (a) `SyncPropose` in a loop of at most 500 calls ends with
     `errors.Is(err, ErrDiskFull)`;
   - (b) `OnLogDBDiskFull` fired exactly once, WALDir = configured WALDir,
     MaxBytes = 1<<20, Shards contains 1;
   - (c) `OnShardFailed` never fired;
   - (d) `HealthCheck` reports `DiskOK` false;
   - (e) after Close and a restart on the same WALDir with
     `MaxWALDiskSize` 0, a proposal succeeds and every proposal
     acknowledged before the refusal is applied.
2. `pkg/config/hostconfig.go`: the `MaxWALDiskSize` comment says it
   bounds the filesystem usage of the volume holding the WAL, not the WAL
   directory size.
3. Per divergence from the docs: a failing `test:` commit, then a `fix:`
   commit in pkg/host.go.
   - (c) failed: the WAL's own monitor refused a `SaveState` with
     `waldb: disk full`, which trips the shard failure latch. Fix: a
     host-side proposal gate (`newProposalDiskGate`) samples the WAL volume
     every 10 proposals at 90%, below the WAL's 95% / every 100 writes, and
     refuses with `ErrDiskFull` first; `OnLogDBDiskFull` fires once,
     whichever monitor trips first. The latch is unchanged.
     docs/log-storage/user.md states when the shard still fails (usage jumps
     past 95% between samples; followers and catch-up writes, which are not
     proposals). The test's UsedBytes floor moves to 90%.
   - Review: one publisher (`diskFullState`) derives the flag from the
     newest sample of either monitor; a newer WAL sample below 90% clears a
     stale gate full state, and `HealthCheck` reads that flag
     (`TestDiskGate_NewerHealthyWALSampleClearsStaleGateFull`).

## Out of scope

- Recovery below the threshold (`OnRecovery`) and the warning callback;
  waldb unit tests cover the monitor's transitions.
- Non-Linux disk usage implementations.
- Any wire or on-disk format change.
