# Plan: #912 — Host metrics collector shard lifecycle

Tracked under #744 (v1.0.0 bar test gap).

## Objective

pkg/metrics.go and docs/observability/user.md say a `MetricsCollector`
passed with `WithMetrics` gets `RegisterShard(shardID)` when the shard is
started, before any per-shard sample, and `UnregisterShard(shardID)` when it
is stopped; docs/observability/architecture.md says the Prometheus collector
drops the shard's per-shard series on `UnregisterShard`. No Host test checks
this: every Host test passes `NoOpMetricsCollector{}`.

## Slices

1. `pkg/host_metrics_shard_lifecycle_test.go` on a transportless Host
   (`WithMemoryLogDB`, `WithoutTransport`, `WithoutEncryption`,
   `WithMetrics(c)`):
   - (a) a recording collector sees `RegisterShard(1)` once after
     StartShard, with no per-shard latency sample for shard 1 before it;
   - (b) after `SyncPropose`, at least one `ObserveProposalLatency(1)`
     recorded after `RegisterShard(1)`;
   - (c) `StopShard(1)` gives exactly one `UnregisterShard(1)`, and a
     second shard 2 on the same Host gets no `UnregisterShard(2)`;
   - (d) StartShard(1) again gives a second `RegisterShard(1)`;
   - (e) with `NewPrometheusMetricsCollectorWithConfig(PerShardMetrics)`,
     the `quicraft_raft_elections_total` series for shard_id "1" exists after
     StartShard and leader election, and is gone after StopShard.
2. Per divergence from the docs: a failing test commit, then a fix commit in
   pkg/host.go (or a doc fix in docs/observability/user.md if the doc is
   wrong).

## Out of scope

- Other collector methods, sampled collector, transport metrics.
- Any change to wire or on-disk formats.
