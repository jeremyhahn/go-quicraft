# #787: StaleRead answers without quorum and SyncRead does not

Objective: pin, on an in-process three-replica shard, that a replica which
has lost its quorum still serves StaleRead from its applied state and that
SyncRead refuses within its deadline, with no goroutine left after Close.

Slices:
1. This plan.
2. pkg/staleread_no_quorum_test.go: TestStaleReadNoQuorum starts three
   hosts (mTLS, loopback), proposes entries, waits for every replica to
   apply them, closes two hosts, then asserts on the survivor that
   StaleRead returns the applied count within 1s and SyncRead with a 500ms
   deadline fails with ErrTimeout or ErrCanceled (errors.Is), and that the
   goroutine count returns to its baseline after the last Close.
   Doc comments of StaleRead and SyncRead (pkg/host.go) are corrected if
   they do not state this. A bug found gets a failing test commit, then a fix.

Out of scope: wire or on-disk format changes; FollowerRead/LeaseRead;
the integration linearizability suite (already covers SyncRead with quorum).
