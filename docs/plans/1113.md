# Row #1113: membership-changes

## Objective

Row `membership-changes` of docs/SPEC.md has one branch
(`row/1113-membership-changes`), one session and one PR that closes #1113.
The row's open per-issue PRs are folded into this branch from current main
and made to pass together. Their per-issue docs/plans files are not carried.

## Slices (one commit per folded PR, in this order)

1. #984 (issue #946): pkg/replica_role_test.go. The ReplicaRole constants
   equal the raft role strings ShardInfo copies; ReplicaProgress marshals its
   role as voter, observer or witness.
2. #949 (issue #983): pkg/snapshot_stop_midsave_test.go,
   test/integration/e2e/grow_shrink_test.go. A shard grown from one host to
   five and shrunk back to one keeps every acknowledged write, one leader.
3. #923 (issue #954): pkg/membership_changed_events_test.go.
   OnMembershipChanged fires on every replica once per applied membership
   change and never for an ordinary proposal.
4. #916 (issue #971): docs/membership-changes/user.md,
   pkg/example_membership_test.go. The guide's flows run as checked examples.
5. #897 (issue #944): pkg/host_async_membership_test.go. RequestAddNode,
   RequestAddObserver, RequestAddWitness and RequestRemoveNode complete
   through their RequestState with the sync form's effect.
6. #893 (issue #940): pkg/host_ordered_config_change_test.go, plus the
   OrderedConfigChange doc/warning change in pkg/config/config.go and the
   matching text in docs/multi-group-host/{api,engineering}.md and
   docs/raft-consensus/engineering.md. A second membership change while one
   is uncommitted is refused with ErrRejected; OrderedConfigChange false
   behaves as documented.
7. #880 (issue #932): pkg/host_witness_policy_async_test.go.
   RequestUpdateWitnessPolicy completes through its RequestState and refuses
   a violated policy or a stale index.
8. #876 (issue #928): pkg/messagehandler_drop_reasons_test.go. Messages from
   a removed replica, a removed node's identity, or addressed to another
   replica are dropped and counted under their documented reasons.

## Out of scope

- Any non-test code change beyond the config.go text carried from #893.
- The per-issue docs/plans files of the folded PRs.
- Closing the folded PRs (the coordinator does that once this row lands).
