# #793: every proposal entry point rejects a payload over the configured limit

Objective: one table test over every exported `*Host` method that submits a
proposal — `Propose`, `SyncPropose`, `ProposeWithSession`,
`SyncProposeWithSession` (there is no batch variant on `*Host`) — with a
payload one byte over and exactly at `effectiveMaxPayload`.

Slice 1 (`pkg/proposal_payload_limit_test.go`, `TestPayloadLimit_*`):
- limits: MaxProposalPayloadSize below MaxEntrySize, MaxEntrySize only
  (MaxProposalPayloadSize 0), and MaxEntrySize below MaxProposalPayloadSize;
- over the limit: error is `*EntryTooLargeError` (errors.Is
  `ErrEntryTooLarge`) with MaxSize equal to the effective limit;
- at the limit: the call succeeds and the entry is applied.

Out of scope: `*config.MaxProposalPayloadSizeExceededError` is a config-time
validation error (covered in pkg/config), not a proposal-time error; the
leader-exec path (pkg/leaderexec.go) has its own tests. A defect found gets a
failing test commit, then the fix.
