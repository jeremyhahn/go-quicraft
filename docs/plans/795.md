# #795: LeaderID and ShardInfo report the new leader after the leader stops

Objective: a pkg/ unit test proves that, after the leader's Host stops, every
surviving replica's `LeaderIDAndTerm` and `ShardInfo` agree on one new leader
at a term above the old one, within 10 election timeouts, and that no survivor
reports the stopped replica at the new term.

Existing coverage (not duplicated): `test/integration/host/leader_ready_test.go`
`TestLeaderReady_Failover` (readiness events) and
`test/integration/e2e/failover_test.go` `TestE2E_Failover_ProposalAfterFailover`
(writes after failover). Neither reads `LeaderIDAndTerm` or `ShardInfo`.

Slice 1 (this lane): `pkg/leader_failover_info_test.go`,
`TestLeaderFailover_LeaderIDAndShardInfoReportTheNewLeader`, on an in-process
three-host mTLS shard. A defect found gets a failing test commit, then a fix.

Out of scope: wire/on-disk formats, transfer and partition scenarios.
