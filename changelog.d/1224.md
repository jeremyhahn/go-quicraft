### Fixed

- pkg/batch: batch flush failures after hand-off to Raft are now reported Indeterminate. A proposer error that is a `*quicraft.MaybeAppliedError` (`quicraft.MaybeApplied` true, e.g. a leader change, shard stop or persist failure after hand-off) now sets `FlushError.Indeterminate`, so callers no longer treat a batch that may still commit as a definite rejection. `*MaybeAppliedError` gains a `MaybeApplied() bool` marker method. No wire or format change. (#1224)
