### Fixed

- transport: peers with disjoint wire windows now produce a diagnostic naming
  both sides' versions. The early frame-header and batch version refusals
  return typed errors (`FrameVersionError`, `proto.BatchVersionError`, both
  `errors.Is` `ErrUnsupportedVersion`) and are handed to the host's message
  handler, which records and logs a `WireVersionMismatchError` once per peer
  address and version change. Its per-address record is bounded and evicts at
  the bound, so many addresses never suppress the diagnostic for a new peer.
  A batch payload shorter than its fixed header is refused the same way
  once its eight BinVer bytes name a version outside the window; such a
  refusal names no peer maximum (`BatchVersionError.HasMaxBinVer` and
  `WireVersionMismatchError.HasPeerMaxBinVer` are false), since MaxBinVer was
  never read. The refusal is recorded synchronously on the receive path,
  and only its log line goes through the shared diagnostic queue, so a full
  queue or a blocked log handler cannot drop or delay a new peer's record.
  The record's lookup, comparison, eviction and insertion are one step under
  a single lock, so concurrent streams and connections cannot grow it past
  its bound or log one version change twice.
  No wire or format version changed.
