### Fixed

- contrib/dragonboat (#1247): with `Config.OrderedConfigChange` set, every add (`SyncRequestAdd*` and `RequestAdd*`, plain and `WithIdentity`) now binds its `configChangeIndex` with `quicraft.WithBoundConfigChangeIndex()`, so an index of 0 is refused as stale once the membership has changed, as dragonboat refuses it. Two operators who both read `ConfigChangeID` 0 can no longer both commit an add. Unordered shards are unchanged; deletes are not yet bound.
